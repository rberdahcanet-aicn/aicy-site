The film

Laughter, grief and a whole lot of M*THER F*CKERS

Since October 7th, 2023, Rapaport has been on a rollercoaster ride of emotions, from the euphoric highs of finding his Jewish identity to the gut-wrenching lows of being canceled by Hollywood for his outspoken views in support of Israel. This documentary captures the raw, unfiltered truth of his experiences, mixing side-splitting comedy with heartbreaking reality. It's a wild ride, filled with laughter, tears, and a whole lot of m*ther f*ckers.

Synopsis

Am I Cancelled Yet? is a groundbreaking new genre of film that blends stand-up comedy with real-life drama, taking audiences on Michael Rapaport's extraordinary emotional journey since October 7, 2023. At the heart of the film is Rapaport's stand-up show, the narrative thread that pulls us in and out of his story. His sharp observations, outrageous humor, and relentless kvetching provide a running commentary on events as they unfold, while vérité scenes reveal the man behind the microphone.

The film moves seamlessly between the stage and real life: Rapaport visits the sites of the October 7 massacres, meets survivors and hostage families, and discovers a deepening connection to his Jewish identity. Back in the US, his outspoken support for Israel puts his career and reputation under pressure. But the consequences are not his alone.

Beside him throughout is his wife, Kebe, who witnesses firsthand the personal cost of being unapologetically visible. As the couple faces violence both on the street and online, Kebe emerges as the quiet hero of the story, a sensitive, hopeful and deeply grounded presence, genuinely worried for her husband while standing firmly by his side. Behind Michael's loud public voice is a woman navigating fear, love and uncertainty, reminding us that outspoken conviction always has a human cost.

Through its dynamic intercut between comedy and vérité, Am I Cancelled Yet? moves between laughter and grief, bravado and vulnerability, public confrontation and private fear. Funny, raw and deeply human, the film asks what happens when shutting up is NOT an option.

Silence isn't an option.

Biographies

The filmmakers

Featuring

Michael Rapaport

Rapaport is a New York-born actor, director, comedian and podcast host whose career spans more than three decades. He has appeared in more than 60 films, including True Romance, Beautiful Girls, Cop Land, Higher Learning, The Heat, Deep Blue Sea and Mighty Aphrodite, working with filmmakers including Woody Allen, Spike Lee and John Singleton.

His extensive television credits include Friends, Prison Break, Boston Public, Justified, Atypical and Only Murders in the Building, as well as numerous guest appearances.

As a director, Rapaport made his documentary debut with Beats, Rhymes & Life: The Travels of A Tribe Called Quest, an acclaimed portrait of the influential hip-hop group that premiered at the Sundance Film Festival. He later directed ESPN's 30 for 30 documentary When the Garden Was Eden, earning a Sports Emmy Award.

Rapaport has also maintained a career in stand-up comedy and has become a distinctive voice in sports and pop culture through his long-running I AM RAPAPORT: STEREO PODCAST. Known for his unmistakable New York energy, outspoken personality and candid approach, he has built a singular career spanning acting, filmmaking, comedy, podcasting and sports culture.

Director

Ruth Berdah-Canet

Ruth Berdah-Canet is an award-winning documentary filmmaker whose poetic, deeply human films find beauty and intimacy in profoundly painful stories of exile, homelessness and cultural identity. Born in France, she has built an international career spanning Japan, New York, where she lived for 17 years, and Israel.

Her first feature documentary screened at festivals across North America and Europe and won Best Documentary at the Chelsea Film Festival in New York.

Following October 7, Berdah-Canet turned her filmmaking toward the Israeli hostages and their families, creating films and international campaigns with the Hostages and Missing Families Forum. Her work with the Forum earned her a Golden Cactus Award for Best Ad in 2026. It was through this work that she met Michael Rapaport.

Their collaboration led to Am I Cancelled Yet?, her latest feature documentary, exploring Rapaport's outspoken support for Israel and the personal and professional consequences of standing up for what you believe in.

Behind the camera

The team

Editor

Yael Tikalsky

Yael Tikalsky is an Israeli film editor and storyteller with over seven years of experience across documentary, television, and digital media. Her work includes projects for the Hostages and Missing Families Forum, television productions including New Love, and A Letter to David, winner of the 2025 Ophir Award for Best Feature-Length Documentary. She is an Avid Media Composer-certified editor and a member of the Israeli Editors Association.

Cinematographer

Emil Swaeid

Emil Swaeid is an Israeli cinematographer and visual storyteller with an extensive career in documentary and television. His credits include the documentary Painting Homeland (Tziyur Moledet), The Institute, The Good One, Never Too Late, MasterChef, Survivor, and Love Island Israel. His work combines a strong visual instinct with the ability to capture intimate, spontaneous moments.

Composer

Argov

Argov is a Tel Aviv-based composer and music producer rooted in hip-hop, with a sound spanning soulful boom bap, lo-fi, trap, and drill. With over seven years of experience and 260+ tracks to his name, his work includes collaborations with Freddie Gibbs, Earl Sweatshirt, Lord Apex, and Mach-Hommy.

Sound design

Rotem Dror

Rotem Dror is an award-winning Israeli sound designer and musician, and a three-time winner of the Israeli Documentary Forum Award for Best Sound Design. She has worked on hundreds of films, including Beaufort, Jellyfish, Precious Life, and Muranow, as well as acclaimed television and documentary projects. A former member of the Israeli rock band Girafot, she also teaches sound design at Minshar School of Art and Tel Aviv University.

Associate producer

Ananey / Paramount

Ananey Studios, a Paramount-Skydance company, is an award-winning Israeli production studio creating bold original content for local and international audiences. Its work spans documentaries, drama, thrillers, kids and teen series, animation and lifestyle, with productions reaching platforms including Netflix, HBO, Nickelodeon and MTV.

The production company

About Chosen Films

Chosen Films is a production company founded by filmmaker Ruth Berdah-Canet and actor and comedian Michael Rapaport, created to bring Jewish and Israeli stories to a wider audience. Born from a desire to move beyond the constant conversation around antisemitism, Chosen Films puts the focus on what deserves to be seen and celebrated: creativity, culture, humor, resilience, and the richness of Jewish life.

Through bold, entertaining and deeply human films, Chosen Films aims to present a positive light on the Jewish and Israeli global narrative, open conversations, challenge stereotypes, and share Jewish and Israeli voices with audiences around the world.
